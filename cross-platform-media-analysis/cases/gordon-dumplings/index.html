6. What this case actually tells us

  1. Respect is the shared core; the lens differs.

    Both audiences converge on the same judgment — Gordon is admirably humble, the chefs admirably strict — but YouTube states it as character praise while Bilibili performs it through faces, craft talk, and persona debate.

  2. The translation thread is YouTube-specific.

    11% of YouTube comments forensically reconstruct the 45/100 score. Bilibili, watching a Chinese-titled re-upload, has no such thread — the "foreign" mediation is invisible to them.

  3. Professional depth lives in Bilibili replies.

    A single self-credentialed chef anchors 277 replies of technique exchange. YouTube's professional comments are shorter and fewer (7%), outweighed by character discussion.

  4. Clean language split, same caveats.

    Unlike the sailing case, platform and language coincide — yet language still does not establish who these viewers are. Thread structure (replies vs top-level) remains a confound for any cultural reading.
