# Coding notes: Gordon dumplings case

## YouTube (100 top-level comments, Top sort)

One primary topic per comment. Categories describe what the comment is
primarily about, not sentiment. When a comment touches two categories,
the more specific or emphasized one wins.

- **humility**: Gordon being humble, a willing student, taking criticism well,
  lifelong learning. Includes mild criticism that he shows pretentiousness
  (rank 60) since it is still about his humility posture.
- **chef_standards**: Head chef / Chinese chefs not lowering standards for a
  celebrity or cameras; not intimidated.
- **score**: The 45/100 vs 5/10 discrepancy and translator sugar-coating.
- **language**: Cantonese observed, translators, Mandarin interpreter,
  language jokes.
- **professional**: Culinary technique: dim sum subtlety/balance, dumpling
  appearance, training years, difficulty of mastery.
- **chef_persona**: Head chef's demeanor (calm, intimidating), presence,
  and appearance (handsome/cute) comments.
- **mutual_respect**: Explicit respect for both sides in one comment.
- **other**: Meta (video age), quote jokes, unclear.

All 100 comments are English. Usernames are omitted from normalized data;
source URLs identify public comments.

## Limitations

Single analyst, no second coder. Bilibili hot endpoint exposed only 3 roots
(same access limit as the sailing case). YouTube sample is Top sort only.
Like counts are engagement snapshots, not population estimates. The Bilibili
video (10:38) is substantially longer than the YouTube clip (4:12); the pair
shares the program, premise, and judged-by-Chinese-chefs structure, but exact
edit equivalence is unverified.
